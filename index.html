How to play

玩法
1

Pick a sport and a level.揀一種運動同一個關卡。

2

Read the word on the ball. Tap Hear it to hear it first.睇波上面嘅字。㩒「聽一次」可以先聽。

3

Take a big breath. Hold the SPACE bar (or the yellow button) and say it loud and slow.深呼吸。按住空白鍵（或者黃色大掣），大聲、慢慢講。

4

Let go. Said it well: GOAL! Nearly there: it hits the post, and the game tells you how close you were. Missed: try again. You get as many tries as you like.放手。講得好：入波！差少少：中柱，遊戲話你知有幾成。射歪咗：再試，試幾多次都得。

5

Five balls make a level. Every try wins coins. Finishing a level wins gold and diamonds.五個波一關。每次試都有金幣，過關有金同鑽石。

6

Spend them in the Store: an orange ball, a watermelon ball, a gold ball, night lights, Yuet Wah College, even a stadium on Mars.喺商店買橙波、西瓜波、金波、夜場燈光、粵華中學球場，仲有火星球場！

7

Cups: a new Sunday Cup every week, the Solar System Cup on the last day of every month, and a World Tour of five Cups from Macau to an alien planet. Double coins and big prizes.盃賽：每個星期日有主日盃，每個月最後一日有太陽系盃，仲有由澳門去到外星球嘅五個世界巡迴賽。雙倍金幣，大獎！

For family 給家人

  • After five strokes, go gently. He may tire quickly, and some days will be harder than others. That's normal. Start with Warm-up or Prayers, keep it short, and finish on a goal he scores. 中過五次風，要慢慢嚟。佢可能好快攰，有啲日子會難啲，係正常嘅。由熱身或者禱文開始，玩短啲，最後一球要入。
  • Use his stronger hand. He can hold the space bar, or tap the yellow button once to start and once to stop. 用佢有力嗰隻手。按住空白鍵，或者㩒一下開始、再㩒一下停。
  • Little and often. 10–15 minutes once or twice a day works better than one long session. The daily goal on the home screen helps. 少量多次：每日一至兩次，每次十至十五分鐘。
  • Sit him upright, with water nearby. Stop if he is tired, frustrated, coughing or dizzy. 坐直，身邊放杯水。攰、唔開心、咳或者頭暈就停。
  • Big breath, then loud and slow. Louder, slower speech is clearer speech. The beat dots help him pace one syllable at a time. 深呼吸，大聲、慢慢講。跟住點點一個字一個字講。
  • When he gets stuck, go up the help ladder: Hear it → Slow beats → Together (say it with him) → then let him try alone. Give him time before helping. 卡住嗰陣：聽一次 → 慢慢拍住講 → 一齊講 → 再自己試。俾時間佢，唔好即刻幫佢講。
  • Praise the effort. Every try earns coins. After a few tries on one word, move on and come back to it another day. 讚佢肯試。試幾次都唔得就跳過，第日再嚟。
  • Familiar words come easier. Counting, prayers and favourite phrases are often the easiest to say after a stroke, so the Prayers stage is open from day one. Add his own words (family names, foods, places) in Family settings. 熟悉嘅說話通常最易講，所以禱文一開始已經開放。可以喺家人設定加佢自己嘅字。
  • The game's listening isn't perfect, especially with slurred speech. Use Coach mode, or tap a different result after any shot. 遊戲聽唔一定準，可以用教練模式，或者射完之後改結果。
  • Show this game to his speech therapist and ask which sounds and words to work on. This game is extra practice; it does not replace therapy. 俾言語治療師睇吓，問佢應該練邊啲音同字。呢個遊戲係額外練習，唔可以代替治療。

Family settings

家人設定

One per line: English | 中文. Family names, favourite foods, places, things his therapist wants him to practise. They become the "My Words" stage. 每行一個：English | 中文。

Paste verses from the translation you like, one per line: Reference | verse. The game uses your wording for those verses wherever they appear, and keeps the Chinese 和合本 beside them. Verses you don't paste stay in the New Heart English Bible. 每行一節：出處 | 經文。

Backup 備份

Save a backup file to keep his progress safe, or to move it to another computer. 存一個備份檔案，或者搬去另一部電腦。

Cups

盃賽

Every Cup is a few rounds of mixed words, sentences and Bible lines, with double coins on every try. Win a Cup the first time for a big prize and a trophy. 每個盃賽都有幾個回合，每次雙倍金幣。第一次贏有大獎同獎盃。

World Tour

世界巡迴賽

Five Cups around the world and beyond. Win one to unlock the next. Each one gets a little harder. 五個盃賽，贏咗一個就開下一個，一個比一個難啲。
