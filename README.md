# Grandpa's Speaking Cup · 爺爺講話盃

A speech practice game for Grandpa, designed by his grandson Joshua.
Say the word on the ball to score: goals, swishes and touchdowns in English and Cantonese.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages address for this repository in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome/Edge, then
Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon.

- Listening uses the phone's or browser's own speech recognition (needs the internet). On iPhone, Dictation must be on (Settings › General › Keyboard).
- Without internet, or without a microphone, a family member can be the coach and tap Goal / Almost / Missed.
- Progress is saved on each device. Family settings has a backup button.
- English Bible verses: New Heart English Bible (public domain). Chinese: 和合本 (public domain).

This is extra practice and does not replace speech therapy.
