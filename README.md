# Grandpa's Speaking Cup · 爺爺講話盃

A speech practice game for Grandpa, designed by his grandson Joshua.
Say the word on the ball to score: goals, swishes and touchdowns in English and Cantonese.

**Play: https://jnanayogi33.github.io/speaking-cup/**. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer, Android phone or tablet, and Safari on iPhone or iPad. Nothing to install and no sign-in.

- **Hands-free (the usual setting):** the game says the word, listens by itself, scores, tries again and moves on. No buttons to hold, so it suits someone who can't use their hands well. Tap anywhere to talk again or carry on. It pauses if it hears nothing twice.
- **First time:** tap Start, and when the browser asks about the microphone, choose **Allow while visiting the site** (Chrome/Edge) so it never asks again.
- **iPhone/iPad:** Dictation must be on (Settings › General › Keyboard › Enable Dictation). Share › Add to Home Screen gives an app icon.
- Listening uses the browser's own speech recognition, so it needs the internet. Firefox, Brave and Opera can't listen; there, a family member can be the coach and tap Goal / Almost / Missed.
- Progress is saved in that browser on that device. Family settings has a backup file.
